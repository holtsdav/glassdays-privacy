Glassdays Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 10, 2026

Glassdays is designed to keep countdown data on your device. The developer does not run analytics, advertising, tracking, crash reporting, or user-account systems in Glassdays.

Data Stored On Device

Glassdays stores countdowns, folders, notification preferences, display preferences, selected images, and widget data locally on your device and in the app's shared app-group container so the app and widget can show your countdowns.

If you choose a photo from your photo library, the selected image is stored locally for countdown or folder backgrounds. Glassdays does not upload your selected photo-library images to the developer.

Optional iCloud Sync

Glassdays Pro can optionally synchronize your countdowns, folders, selected images, and related settings through your private iCloud account using Apple CloudKit. This keeps the same information available on your Apple devices. The developer does not operate an account system or use this data for analytics, advertising, or tracking.

You can turn off iCloud Sync in Glassdays Settings at any time. You can delete individual countdowns or folders in the app, and deleting the app removes its local data. iCloud data is handled through your Apple account and Apple’s CloudKit service.

Calendar Access

If you choose “Add to Apple Calendar,” Glassdays asks for calendar access and creates, updates, or removes the calendar events you choose. The event title, date, recurrence, and selected time zone are sent to your device’s Calendar store. You can revoke access at any time in iOS Settings.

Network Features

Glassdays uses network requests for these features:

  • Collections presets: the app downloads gallery data and related preset images from the developer's GitHub-hosted CountdownJSON repository.
  • Unsplash search: when you search Unsplash, your search query is sent to the Unsplash API and Unsplash image URLs are loaded directly from Unsplash's CDN. When you choose an Unsplash photo, Glassdays notifies Unsplash of that download event and shows linked photographer and Unsplash attribution.
  • Optional support links: Ko-fi links open outside the app.
  • Optional support email: if you send feedback or a bug report, the email content is sent by your mail provider to the developer's support address.

The developer does not use these requests for advertising or tracking. Third-party services may process standard network information, such as IP address, device/browser/network metadata, server logs, and the content of requests sent to them, under their own privacy policies.

Notifications

Glassdays schedules local notifications on your device. Notification settings and offsets are stored locally. The developer does not receive notification content.

Widgets

Countdown widgets read countdown data from the shared local app-group store. The widget does not send countdown data to the developer.

Tracking

Glassdays does not track you across apps or websites and does not use the advertising identifier.

Contact

If you contact the developer for support, the developer receives the information you choose to include in your message.

Retention and Your Choices

Glassdays keeps local app data until you delete it in the app or remove the app. Support emails are retained only as long as reasonably needed to respond to and resolve your request. You may stop using optional network features, notifications, calendar access, and iCloud Sync at any time through Glassdays or iOS Settings.

For privacy questions or a deletion request relating to a support message, contact 8-kapsel-guertel@icloud.com.

Third-Party Services

Apple’s services, Unsplash, GitHub, Ko-fi, and your mail provider have their own privacy practices. Their handling of information is governed by their respective policies.
